PSYC 310 • Organizational Psychology

Literature Review Paragraph Refinement (APA 7th Edition)

Focus: Academic Tone, Conciseness & Evidence Integration
Original Student Draft Grade Risk: C+ (Wordy / Weak Citation)

"Nowadays a lot of companies are letting people work from home and this has both good and bad things about it. In a study by Smith (2022), he talked about how workers feel less stressed when they don't have to drive to work every day. However, some people feel lonely and left out of team meetings which hurts how well the company does over time. So managers need to figure out a better way to communicate with remote workers."

Rubric Issue 1: Informal phrasing ("Nowadays," "a lot," "good and bad things").

Rubric Issue 2: Missing synthesis of mechanisms behind isolation & performance.

Edited & Rubric-Aligned Version Target Score: 96% (A)

"The widespread adoption of remote work arrangements presents a dual organizational dynamic, simultaneously reducing commuter-related burnout while straining team cohesion. Empirical findings indicate that telecommuting significantly lowers daily cortisol levels and increases perceived autonomy (Smith & Patel, 2022). Conversely, prolonged physical separation diminishes spontaneous knowledge sharing and fosters professional isolation, which correlates with reduced organizational commitment (Chen, 2023). Consequently, leadership must implement structured asynchronous communication protocols to preserve collaborative output without eroding employee flexibility."

Editor Note 1: Replaced conversational fillers with precise scholarly vocabulary.

Editor Note 2: Added comparative synthesis (Smith & Patel, 2022; Chen, 2023) for critical depth.

NUR 305 • Adult Health Clinical

Acute Decompensated Heart Failure (ADHF) — Priority Care Plan Excerpt

Format: NANDA-I / NIC / NOC Clinical Matrix
1. Assessment Data
  • Subjective: Reports orthopnea (3-pillow), dyspnea on minimal exertion.
  • Objective: SpO2 89% on room air, RR 26/min, bilateral basilar crackles, +3 pitting pedal edema, BNP 1,420 pg/mL.
2. Nursing Diagnosis

Impaired Gas Exchange related to alveolar-capillary membrane fluid accumulation secondary to left ventricular dysfunction, as evidenced by SpO2 89%, tachypnea, and bibasilar crackles.

3. Priority Interventions
  • Place patient in High-Fowler’s position immediately.
  • Administer supplemental O2 via nasal cannula to maintain SpO2 ≥ 92%.
  • Administer IV Furosemide 40 mg as ordered; monitor strict I&O and serum K+.
4. Evidence Rationale

High-Fowler’s reduces venous return (preload) to the heart and maximizes diaphragmatic excursion. Loop diuretics decrease intravascular volume overload, while potassium monitoring prevents hypokalemia-induced dysrhythmias (Ackley et al., 2023).

STAT 220 • Applied Biostatistics

Two-Sample Independent t-Test & APA Results Reporting

Software: SPSS / R Studio Output Breakdown
1

State Hypotheses (α = 0.05)

H₀: μ₁ = μ₂ (No mean difference in exam scores between spaced-practice and cramming groups).
H₁: μ₁ ≠ μ₂ (A statistically significant difference exists between study methods).

2

Test Statistic & Degrees of Freedom

Levene’s test confirms equal variances (p = .412).
Spaced Practice (n = 32): M = 88.4, SD = 5.2 | Cramming (n = 30): M = 79.1, SD = 6.1
Computed t(60) = 6.47, p < .001, 95% CI [6.42, 12.18], Cohen’s d = 1.64.

Step 3: APA Write-Up Ready to Submit

"An independent-samples t-test revealed that students utilizing spaced practice (M = 88.40, SD = 5.20) scored significantly higher on the final assessment than those utilizing massed cramming (M = 79.10, SD = 6.10), t(60) = 6.47, p < .001, d = 1.64. The null hypothesis is rejected."

Includes annotated SPSS syntax & assumption checks.
